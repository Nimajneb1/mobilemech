Auto electrical diagnostics

Electrical fault finding at your location.

Modern vehicles rely on multiple electronic control systems, sensors and electrical circuits. When a vehicle will not start, the battery keeps going flat, warning lights appear or an electrical function stops working, accurate testing can help avoid unnecessary parts replacement.

Our mobile auto electrical service can assess common starting and charging faults, battery condition, electrical warning lights and other vehicle electrical issues. Where a repair requires workshop equipment, specialist programming or facilities that are not mobile-friendly, we will explain the next step.

  • Battery and charging system testing
  • Starter and no-start electrical checks
  • Electrical fault finding
  • Sensor and warning light diagnostics
  • Vehicle electrical system checks
