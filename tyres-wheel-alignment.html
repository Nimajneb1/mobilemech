Tyre and alignment support

Keep tyres wearing correctly and the vehicle tracking properly.

Uneven tyre wear, steering pull, vibration or a steering wheel that is not centred can indicate a tyre or wheel alignment issue. Tyres are a key safety component, so the correct tyre size, condition, pressure and alignment setup matter.

For tyre replacement, balancing and wheel alignment, Mobile Mech can help coordinate the appropriate Auckland service-centre work. We can also inspect related vehicle concerns and advise when a mechanical or suspension issue may be contributing to abnormal tyre wear.

  • Tyre replacement support
  • Wheel balancing
  • Wheel alignment coordination
  • Uneven tyre wear assessment
  • Steering pull and vibration enquiries

Tyre concerns

Common tyre and alignment problems.

Tyre symptoms can sometimes be caused by alignment, suspension or other mechanical issues, so the cause matters as much as the tyre itself.

Wear

Uneven tyre wear

Cupping, shoulder wear or uneven tread can warrant an alignment and suspension assessment.

Steering

Vehicle pulls to one side

Steering pull can be related to alignment, tyre condition or other mechanical factors.

Vibration

Steering wheel vibration

Balancing, tyre condition and wheel or suspension issues can all be relevant when vibration occurs.
