Mobile Mechanic Auckland for Car Servicing, Repairs & Diagnostics

Mobile Mech provides professional mobile mechanic services across Auckland, bringing car servicing, vehicle repairs, maintenance and diagnostics to your home, workplace or roadside. If you are searching for a mobile mechanic in Auckland, a mechanic near you, mobile car servicing or on-site car repairs, we make it easier to get your vehicle checked and repaired without a trip to a workshop.

Our mobile automotive services include routine car servicing, mechanical repairs, vehicle diagnostics, auto electrical fault finding, battery testing and replacement, pre-purchase inspections, fleet servicing, oil leak repair and check engine light diagnosis. We work with most makes and models, including Japanese, European and American vehicles, using professional diagnostic equipment to identify faults and recommend the right repair.

Oil Leak Repair & Engine Diagnostics in Auckland

Noticed oil on the driveway, an oil smell or signs of an engine oil leak? Our mobile mechanic can inspect the vehicle, identify the likely source and advise on the appropriate oil leak repair, including common gasket and seal issues where the repair can be completed on-site. If your engine check light is on, we can also carry out a diagnostic scan and fault-finding assessment to help identify the cause.
