Oil Leak Repair & Engine Diagnostics in Auckland

Oil on the driveway, an oil smell or signs of an engine oil leak can indicate a worn seal, gasket or another issue. Our mobile mechanic can inspect the vehicle, identify the likely source and advise on the appropriate oil leak repair, including repairs that can be completed on-site where practical.

Fault finding

Engine Check Light On

If your engine check light is on, we can carry out a diagnostic scan and fault-finding assessment to help identify the cause. We can check common engine and electrical fault codes and explain the findings before recommending the appropriate repair or next step.
