Headlight restoration Auckland

Headlight restoration for faded lenses

Faded or cloudy headlight lenses can reduce clarity and affect how effectively the headlights project light. Mobile Mech can help restore suitable plastic headlight lenses across Auckland.

Headlight lens restoration

Restore clearer headlights.

Headlight restoration is suitable for many vehicles where the outer plastic lens has become cloudy, yellowed or heavily oxidised. The process focuses on improving the condition and clarity of the lens surface rather than replacing the complete headlight assembly.

If you are searching for headlight restoration Auckland or a mobile headlight restoration service near you, send photos of the headlights and vehicle details so the condition can be assessed before booking. Results depend on lens condition, previous repairs and the depth of the damage.

  • Cloudy and yellowed headlight lenses
  • Surface oxidation and fading
  • Lens cleaning and refinishing
  • Improved visual clarity
  • Assessment before work begins
