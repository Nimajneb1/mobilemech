Professional vehicle body and paint repairs

Restore damaged panels and paintwork with a clear repair plan.

Mobile Mech provides panel and paint support for Auckland drivers dealing with scratches, scuffs, bumper damage, damaged panels, faded paint and larger refinishing jobs. We assess the affected area, consider the level of preparation required and recommend the most suitable repair approach rather than treating every job the same. Our panel and paint service is aimed at achieving a clean, durable finish that blends with the existing vehicle colour and restores the appearance of the affected area.

For suitable jobs, we pick up and drop off the vehicle, making the process easier for customers who cannot leave a vehicle at a repair facility. We can also discuss full resprays and larger cosmetic repairs where the job requires more extensive preparation, paint application and finishing. Send through vehicle details and photos when booking so we can assess the damage and provide the right next step.

  • Scratch, scuff and bumper paint repairs
  • Sun-faded and weathered paint refinishing
  • Damaged panel preparation, repair and repainting
  • Colour matching and professional refinishing
  • Full or partial vehicle resprays
  • Vehicle pick-up and drop-off for suitable repairs
