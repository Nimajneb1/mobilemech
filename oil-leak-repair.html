Oil leak repair Auckland

Oil leak diagnosis and repair

Oil on the driveway, an oil smell or a visible engine oil leak can have several causes. Mobile Mech can inspect the vehicle, identify the likely source and advise on the appropriate repair.

Engine oil leak diagnosis

Find where the oil is coming from.

An engine oil leak can come from a gasket, seal, filter area, sump, rocker or valve cover, oil cooler, drain plug or another component. The visible oil may also travel from the original source, so finding the actual leak point is important before replacing parts.

Our mobile mechanic can carry out an oil leak inspection at your Auckland location and explain whether the repair is suitable to complete on-site. If the repair requires workshop equipment or extensive disassembly, we will explain the recommended next step.

  • Engine oil leak inspection
  • Oil smell and visible leak assessment
  • Gasket and seal leak diagnosis
  • Oil filter, cooler and sump-area checks
  • Repair advice and quote before work

Engine
