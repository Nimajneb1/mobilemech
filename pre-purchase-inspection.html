Before you buy

Get a vehicle checked before you commit.

Buying a used car, van or SUV can involve more than checking the exterior. A pre-purchase inspection can look at the vehicle condition, visible mechanical issues, warning lights, fluid leaks, tyres, brakes, suspension and other accessible components, with diagnostic checks where appropriate.

If you are searching for a pre-purchase car inspection Auckland or a mobile vehicle inspection near you, we can arrange an assessment at the vehicle location where practical. The inspection is intended to provide useful information about the vehicle condition; it is not a guarantee of future reliability.

  • Visual mechanical and body condition checks
  • Fluid leaks and maintenance concerns
  • Tyres, brakes and suspension observations
  • Warning lights and diagnostic assessment where suitable
  • Clear findings to help inform your purchase decision
