EV battery service

Understand the health of your EV battery.

The high-voltage battery is one of the most important components in an electric vehicle. State of health, commonly abbreviated to SOH, is used to describe battery condition compared with its original capacity. It can provide useful information when maintaining, buying or selling an EV.

Mobile Mech can discuss EV battery servicing and check whether AVILOO state-of-health testing is available for your vehicle. Availability depends on vehicle compatibility and the testing requirements, so confirm the exact vehicle before booking.
