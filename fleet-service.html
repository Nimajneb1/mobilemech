Mobile fleet care

Designed for busy fleet operators.

Mobile Mech provides comprehensive on-site fleet servicing for Auckland businesses with two or more vehicles. Our package combines routine maintenance with a 30-point safety inspection, vehicle diagnostics, road testing and a written report, helping fleet operators identify maintenance issues before they become unexpected downtime. We service popular fleet utes and commercial vans including Ford Ranger, Ford Transit, Toyota Hilux, Toyota Hiace, Mitsubishi Triton, Nissan Navara, Isuzu D-Max, Volkswagen Amarok, Mercedes-Benz Sprinter and Volkswagen Transporter. Larger fleets can discuss volume pricing and a dedicated fleet labour rate for additional repairs and diagnostic work.

Comprehensive package

What’s included.

Fleet pricing applies for a minimum of two vehicles. Volume reductions are available for larger fleets.

Core service

Oil and filter change, essential fluid top-ups, brake fluid check and hand-brake / drum-brake clean, suspension and steering safety inspection.

System care

Engine flush, fuel system additive (petrol & diesel), systems health check, complete system scan and report, road test and written report.

30-point inspection

Full check covering fluids, filters, belts, battery, lights, brakes, tyres, steering, suspension, CV joints, exhaust and underbody.

Service takes approximately 1.5 hours (minimum 1 hour) and the vehicle must be on level ground. Additional repairs are charged at the fleet labour rate and are not included in the package.
