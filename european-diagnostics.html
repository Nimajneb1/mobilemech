European Car Diagnostics & Repairs Auckland

Mobile Mech provides professional mobile European car diagnostics, servicing and mechanical repairs across Auckland, coming to your home, workplace or roadside. We use professional diagnostic equipment alongside mechanical and electrical fault finding to investigate warning lights, engine faults, ABS and airbag warnings, sensor faults, charging and starting problems, drivability issues, poor running, loss of power and other vehicle faults before recommending repairs.

Where practical, our mobile mechanic can complete the repair on-site, including European vehicle servicing, engine diagnostics, fault-code scanning, auto electrical fault finding, battery and charging-system checks, oil leak diagnosis and repair, check engine light diagnosis and general mechanical repairs. We specialise in Volkswagen, Audi, BMW and Mercedes-Benz and also service Japanese and American makes including Ford, Holden, Toyota, Nissan, Mazda, Mitsubishi, Subaru, Honda, Suzuki, Hyundai and Kia.

  • European vehicle diagnostic scanning
  • Engine management and warning-light diagnosis
  • Auto electrical fault finding
  • ABS, airbag and sensor diagnostics
  • On-site mechanical repairs where practical
  • Volkswagen, Audi, BMW and Mercedes-Benz focus
