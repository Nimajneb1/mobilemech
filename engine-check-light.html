Engine warning light

Diagnose the cause instead of guessing.

A check engine light can be triggered by many different faults, from sensor and ignition issues to emissions, fuel, electrical or other engine-management problems. A diagnostic scan can retrieve relevant fault codes, but proper diagnosis also requires interpreting those codes alongside symptoms and testing.

Our mobile engine diagnostics service is available across Auckland for vehicles that have a check engine light on, intermittent warning lights or related running problems. We can explain the findings and recommend repair work or further testing where needed.

  • Check engine light scanning
  • Engine fault code assessment
  • Misfire and running fault enquiries
  • Sensor and electrical fault diagnosis
  • Repair recommendations based on findings
