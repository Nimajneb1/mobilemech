Vehicle diagnostics

Find the fault before replacing parts.

Mobile car diagnostics can help when a warning light appears, the vehicle runs poorly, an electrical system behaves unexpectedly or a fault is intermittent. We can carry out a diagnostic scan and fault-finding assessment at your location where practical.

A scan identifies stored or current fault information; it does not automatically prove which component has failed. We use the codes alongside symptoms, vehicle information and testing to narrow down the cause and recommend the appropriate repair or next diagnostic step.

  • Engine check light diagnostics
  • OBD diagnostic scanning
  • Electrical fault finding
  • Starting and charging system checks
  • Fault code assessment and repair advice
